The Solution

Four Data-Driven Interventions That Reversed the Decline

Sails Software designed and deployed a comprehensive analytics and intelligence platform addressing each challenge directly — transforming blind decision-making into a data-driven competitive advantage.

Real-Time Dashboards

Custom-built dashboards gave leadership an instant, unified view of sales performance, shipping issues, customer feedback, and profit margins — eliminating the blind spots that paralyzed decision-making for 18 months.

ML-Powered Re-Pricer

Using historical data, demand patterns, and competitor trends, Sails Software built a machine learning Re-Pricer tool to dynamically win the Amazon Buy Box — turning pricing from guesswork into a precise, data-driven competitive advantage.

Predictive Analytics

Through time-series analysis, the platform forecasts product demand and recommends proactive inventory adjustments ahead of seasonal peaks — replacing reactive firefighting with strategic planning.

Cross-Selling Strategy

Using customer segmentation, Sails Software identified product bundles and upsell paths to boost average order value — expanding revenue per customer without increasing acquisition costs.
